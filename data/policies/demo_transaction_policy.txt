FinGuard AI - Demonstration Transaction Investigation Policy

DOCUMENT TYPE: Synthetic demonstration policy
STATUS: Not an official banking regulation or legal standard.

1. Purpose
This document provides example investigation guidance for the FinGuard AI demonstration system.

2. Transaction Risk
A transaction should be reviewed when multiple unusual signals are present.
A single unusual signal should not automatically establish fraud.

3. Transfer Transactions
Transfer transactions may receive additional scrutiny because the analyzed demonstration dataset contains a higher concentration of fraud in this transaction category.

4. Balance Anomalies
Unexpected sender or recipient balance changes should be investigated.
Missing or inconsistent information must be treated as unknown rather than proof of fraud.

5. Human Review
High-risk and medium-risk cases should be routed for human review.
The reviewer should inspect transaction details and supporting evidence before making a decision.

6. Decision Restrictions
The system must not automatically freeze accounts, reverse payments, or declare a customer guilty of fraud.

7. Evidence Requirements
Investigation reports must distinguish observed facts, calculated risk signals, and assumptions.
The system must not invent missing transaction details.

8. Escalation
Cases with insufficient evidence, conflicting signals, or failed policy retrieval should be escalated for manual investigation.
